Cyber Kill chain assignment


Reading assignment

The following is a summary of An article by Eric M Hutchins for Lockheed martin

1. Because the kill chain is a chain, mitigating just one phase disrupts the entire attack. While the attackers do have the initiative advantage, the defenders have multiple ways and time points to stop the attack. Therefore attackers do not have an inherent advantage.


2. If an attack is detected early, (such as in the delivery phase), it is important to try to predict what they would have done next, for example, analyzing an email payload, that was caught early, might lead to a discovery of a new software vulnerability. In such a way, the defenders can stay ahead of the attackers.


3. Attackers have patterns, It is important to stay ahead and notice said patterns to prevent further attacks.


4. Even a Zero-day vunerability does not bring a guaranteed success for the attackers, because the kill chain is a chain, the attack can still be prevented in other phases of the attack. Therefore hyperfocusing on simple vunerability detection and mitigation can be counterproductive. It is very important to keep the big picture in sight.
